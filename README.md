# Hi, I'm Tejas Dalvi

**M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence student at Radboud University**  
*Research interests: Brain-inspired AI · Spiking Neural Networks · Agentic AI · Neuromorphic Computing*

I am interested in building and evaluating intelligent systems inspired by principles of biological computation. My current research direction explores how spiking neural networks and biologically inspired learning mechanisms can support adaptive, modular agentic systems.

## Research interests

- **Brain-inspired and neuromorphic AI:** spiking neural networks (SNNs), temporal computation, and learning with spikes.
- **Agentic AI:** modular agents, world models, action evaluation, planning, and adaptive decision-making.
- **Biologically inspired learning:** reward-modulated plasticity, including reward-modulated STDP (R-STDP), and local learning rules.
- **Embodied intelligence:** autonomous systems and multi-agent robotics.

## Current research

### Brain-Inspired Agentic AI
I am investigating an agentic architecture built from modular SNN components. The initial CartPole prototype is intended to study the roles of a **World Model**, **Action Evaluator**, and **Planner**, with R-STDP as a candidate learning mechanism.

**Status:** research prototype in development. Results and capabilities will be documented as they are implemented and evaluated.

- Planned/active topics: next-state prediction, action evaluation, modular planning, adaptation, robustness, and computational cost.
